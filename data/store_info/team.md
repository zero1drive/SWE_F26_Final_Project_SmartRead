# The team

smartread is built and run by Group Z, a team of students in a university software engineering course in fall 2026.

The team wrote the requirements, designed the store, and built every feature: the catalog, the account and payment rules, the reviews and moderation system, the face login, the AI clerk and the reading journal.

The store manager account belongs to the team and is used to add books, manage customers and read the finance report.

You can reach the team at the front desk during opening hours, or by asking the clerk to pass on a message.
