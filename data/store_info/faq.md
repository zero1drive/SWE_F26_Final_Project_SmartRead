# Frequently asked questions

How do I open an account? Click "Log in", then fill in the "New here?" form. You start as a member with a balance of 0 dollars.

How do I log in with my face? After logging in with your password once, open your account page and enrol your face. The camera takes about 20 pictures and trains a small recognition model. Next time, choose "Log in with my face" on the login page.

Can I search by picture? Yes. On the catalog page, upload a photo of a book cover or a figure from a book. The AI describes the picture and we show the books that match best.

What is the bookshelf and journal? Every visitor and member has a personal bookshelf with three shelves: want to read, reading and finished. You can write journal entries about each book and ask the AI for a reflection question about what you wrote. Members' shelves are saved to their account. Visitors' shelves last only until the browser tab is closed. Nothing on your shelf is ever made public.

Why was my comment blocked? It contained more than 3 words from our list of banned words. You received one warning.

Who can see the finance report? Only the store manager.
