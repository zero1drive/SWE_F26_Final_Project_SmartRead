# Hours and policies

The store is open from 10 am to 8 pm, Tuesday to Sunday. We are closed on Mondays and on public holidays. The online catalog is open all the time.

Payment works with your account balance. Add funds on your account page, then buy any book with one click. There is no shopping cart; one click buys one copy.

If a book costs more than your balance, the purchase is refused and a fine of 1 dollar is charged to your account. If your balance goes below zero, your account is suspended and you cannot log in. To reopen it, contact the store manager, who will deposit funds and reopen the account once the balance is positive.

Members who make more than 3 purchases, or spend more than 200 dollars, become VIP members automatically. VIP members get 10 percent off every book.

Anyone can rate a book from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Ratings from people who bought the book count double. Comments with rude words have those words replaced by a star; a comment with more than 3 of them is blocked and its author receives a warning. A member with more than 2 warnings is suspended, and a VIP with more than 2 warnings goes back to being a regular member.

Refunds: because our books are digital editions, sales are final. If a book file is damaged, contact us and we will replace it.
