# About smartread

smartread is a small independent bookstore that sells classic books. Every book in our catalog is a public-domain title from Project Gutenberg, so our prices are low and we can stock books that big shops forget about.

The store was started in 2026 by Group Z, a team of software engineering students, as a class project. We wanted a bookshop that feels like a small library in the evening: wooden shelves, warm light and a clerk who remembers what you liked.

We sell eight kinds of books: Literature, Science Fiction, Adventure, Mystery, Horror, Children's books, Science and Poetry. New titles are added by the store manager every week.

Our clerk is an AI assistant that runs on our own computer using Ollama. It never sends your questions to an outside company.
